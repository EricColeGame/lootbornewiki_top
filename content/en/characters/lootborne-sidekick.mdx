## Why Your Lootborne Sidekick Matters More Than You Think

Most players obsess over their own weapon rolls and forget the ally fighting beside them. That's a mistake. A well-built Lootborne sidekick can cover your build's weakest stat, soak damage you can't survive, and turn a failed run into a clean clear. Ignore it, and you'll wonder why your damage feels fine but your survival rate doesn't.

In a loot-driven action RPG, every source of scaling matters, and your companion is one of the few you can fully control without changing your main character. This guide breaks down sidekick roles, pairing logic, upgrade priorities, and the mistakes that quietly cap your progression.

## What Is a Lootborne Sidekick?

A Lootborne sidekick is a persistent AI-controlled ally that fights alongside your main character, scales with its own gear and upgrade path, and can be customized through role selection, ability loadouts, and equipment. Unlike temporary summons or one-off consumables, your sidekick stays with you across runs and grows as you invest in it.

Community reports consistently describe the sidekick as a "second build" rather than a pet. That framing matters: you're not just picking a helper, you're choosing a second stat profile, a second damage type, and a second set of cooldowns that either complement your kit or fight against it.

| Sidekick Archetype | Primary Job | Best Paired With | Main Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bruiser / Frontline | Hold aggro, absorb hits | Squishy ranged or caster builds | Low personal damage |
| Ranged DPS | Consistent chip and pressure | Melee tanks and bruisers | Fragile if unprotected |
| Healer / Support | Sustain, cleanse, revive | Glass-cannon burst builds | Weak solo clear speed |
| Utility / Scavenger | Loot finds, mobility, debuffs | Farming and speed builds | Minimal combat impact |
| Buffer / Enabler | Damage amps, shields, crowd control | Any coordinated team | Timing-dependent value |

The exact names and skill lists attached to these archetypes shift between patches, so treat the table above as a role framework rather than a fixed roster. What stays consistent is the underlying design: each sidekick trades something to give you something else.

### How Sidekicks Scale

In most looter ARPGs, companion scaling comes from three places: the companion's own level, its equipped gear, and any shared progression system that ties it to your account. Player experience suggests that gear contributes the largest share of a sidekick's late-game power, which is why an under-geared companion can feel useless even at max level.

## How to Unlock and Recruit Your Sidekick

Progression steps vary by game version, but the general flow follows a familiar pattern. Verify exact requirements in your current build, since unlock conditions are one of the most commonly patched systems.

| Game Stage | Typical Milestone | What You Should Do |
|---|---|---|
| Early game | Intro quest introduces the companion system | Finish the tutorial chain before grinding |
| Early-mid | First companion slot opens | Test two roles before committing resources |
| Mid game | Upgrade materials become farmable | Start funneling gear into one sidekick |
| Late game | Higher rarity companion gear unlocks | Re-roll stats rather than replacing pieces |
| Endgame | Full ability customization | Build around synergy, not raw stats |

The single most important habit here is **committing early**. Spreading upgrade materials across three companions is the fastest way to end up with three mediocre allies and a character that can't clear the content you're stuck on.

## Best Lootborne Sidekick Roles by Player Build

Pairing is where most players gain or lose the most value. The right companion fixes your build's weakness; the wrong one duplicates your strength.

| Your Build Type | Recommended Sidekick Role | Why It Works |
|---|---|---|
| Melee bruiser, high sustain | Ranged DPS | Adds reach and pressure you lack |
| Ranged glass cannon | Healer / Support or Bruiser | Keeps you alive during burst windows |
| Summoner / minion build | Buffer / Enabler | Multiplies every unit on screen |
| Speed farm / loot build | Utility / Scavenger | Improves clear speed and drop efficiency |
| Crowd-control build | Ranged DPS or Buffer | Converts lockdown into kill pressure |
| Hybrid / off-meta | Bruiser | Stabilizes inconsistent matchups |

A useful rule of thumb: if your build already solves a problem, your sidekick should solve a *different* one. Two defensive companions on a tanky character produces a slow, safe, boring run that still can't kill a boss within the timer.

## Gearing and Leveling Your Sidekick Efficiently

Companion gear is where the biggest power spikes hide, and it's also where players waste the most resources. Here's how to prioritize.

| Priority | Upgrade Action | Impact Level | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Weapon / primary damage item | Very high | Do this first, always |
| 2 | Survival stats (health, resistances) | High | Prevents mid-fight deaths |
| 3 | Ability cooldown reduction | High | More uptime on key skills |
| 4 | Utility stats (movement, pickup radius) | Medium | Farming builds only |
| 5 | Cosmetic and minor rolls | Low | Save for last |

**Actionable tip:** before spending rare upgrade materials, run the same encounter three times with your current setup and note how often your sidekick dies. If it dies more than once per run, your priority is survival stats, not damage.

### Leveling Without Wasting Time

Sidekicks typically gain experience from the same activities that level your character, which means the most efficient path is simply to keep playing content at your level rather than farming low-tier areas. If a dedicated companion XP system exists in your version, check whether it caps per day — community reports often flag hidden daily limits that make marathon grinding pointless.

## Combat Synergy: Getting Real Value From Your Sidekick

Having the right companion is half the job. Commanding it well is the other half.

| Do This | Avoid This | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Position enemies between you and your sidekick | Let your sidekick lead the pull | Fewer unnecessary deaths |
| Save companion cooldowns for elite packs | Burn abilities on trash mobs | Higher uptime when it counts |
| Match damage types to enemy resistances | Ignore elemental weaknesses | Faster kill times |
| Reassign roles for boss fights | Use one loadout for everything | Better matchup coverage |
| Check companion stats after each major gear change | Assume stats auto-update | No surprise power drops |

One of the most underrated habits is **re-specing your Lootborne sidekick before boss attempts**. Trash clearing and boss fighting reward different profiles, and swapping a scavenger build for a sustain build before a hard encounter is often the difference between a two-minute fight and a wipe.

## Common Sidekick Mistakes That Cap Your Progress

- **Treating the companion as a cosmetic.** It's a full stat sheet; gear it like one.
- **Chasing meta lists blindly.** A "top tier" companion that doesn't fix your build's weakness is still a downgrade.
- **Ignoring survivability.** A dead sidekick contributes zero damage, no matter how good its rolls are.
- **Over-investing in multiple companions at once.** Depth beats breadth in almost every loot game.
- **Skipping ability tuning.** Small cooldown and targeting adjustments often outperform raw stat gains.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is the Lootborne sidekick worth upgrading early, or should I wait?**
Upgrade early, but upgrade narrowly. Pick one companion that complements your main build and invest in it consistently. Waiting until endgame usually means you've already cleared content without the power boost that would have made the journey smoother.

**Can I change my Lootborne sidekick's role later?**
In most versions, role and ability changes are reversible, though some upgrade materials are not refundable. Test roles during low-stakes content before committing rare resources, and check whether your version offers a free respec window.

**Which sidekick role is best for solo play?**
For solo runs, a Bruiser or Healer archetype tends to perform best because it solves the two problems solo players face most: incoming damage and sustain. Damage-focused companions shine in groups where someone else is holding aggro.

**Why does my sidekick feel weak at high levels?**
Nine times out of ten, it's gear. Companion power is heavily weighted toward equipped items rather than raw level, so an ally with outdated gear will underperform even at the level cap. Re-check its weapon and defensive stats first.

## Final Thoughts

Your Lootborne sidekick isn't a side character — it's a second build you get to control for free. Pick a role that patches your weaknesses, funnel gear into one companion instead of three, and re-tune it before every hard encounter. Do that, and the runs that used to end in a wipe start ending in a clear.
